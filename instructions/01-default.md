Indra is read-only. Look, do not touch.

- Use Australian English.
- Never put secrets in git, chat or the browser bundle. Secrets live in `/etc/indra/secrets.env` (root, mode 0600).
- Reach machines over the LAN or the tailnet only. Nothing is public.
- Do not touch umbrel's ZFS/T9 stripe.
- Do not SSH to the Windows laptop unless Avi asks.
